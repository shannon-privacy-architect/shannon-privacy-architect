# Hi, I'm Shannon Ramirez

I'm a Zero-Custody Systems Architect focused on building privacy-first tools, infrastructure, and workflows that help people maintain control of their data while still benefiting from modern technologies, including AI when it adds meaningful value.

My work centers on:

- Trust-by-design systems
- Privacy-preserving technologies
- Data governance
- Zero-knowledge proofing
- PII sanitation
- Verifiable workflows

I design solutions that can operate as standalone tools or integrate with existing platforms and workflows, helping organizations adopt new capabilities without replacing the systems they already rely on.

A large part of my work involves enabling people and organizations to use data responsibly without exposing information that does not need to be shared. Whether supporting traditional software or AI-driven workflows, privacy, governance, and user control remain the foundation of everything I build.

I believe technology should serve people, not the other way around. My goal is to build practical solutions that are trustworthy, useful, and grounded in real-world needs.

I approach technology through experimentation, rapid prototyping, and real-world validation. Building and stress-testing systems has given me a practical understanding of where architectures succeed, where they fail, and how they can be improved.
